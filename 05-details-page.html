The First National Dialogue / Meli Maraka Session Was Held in Sacramento, California

MM
Mili Mehwar
Editorial Team
Share:

Following the successful national dialogue sessions held in London, Berlin, Birmingham, and Cologne, the National Dialogue / Meli Maraka was held for the first time in Sacramento, California. A large number of the country's academic, political, religious, social, and cultural figures were in attendance, representing a broad cross-section of Afghan society from across the diaspora.

The session opened with recitation from the Holy Quran, followed by welcoming remarks from the chairman of Mili Mehwar, who emphasised the importance of organised, principled political engagement as the only viable path toward restoring Afghanistan's sovereignty and democratic foundations.

Key Discussions and Working Groups

The dialogue was structured around four working groups, each tasked with developing concrete recommendations in their respective areas:

  • Political governance and the path to an inclusive political settlement
  • Human rights, with particular focus on women's rights and education
  • The role of the Afghan diaspora in national reconstruction
  • Economic sovereignty and development priorities

Each working group presented its findings to the plenary, generating substantive debate and ultimately producing a set of shared recommendations that will be compiled into a formal report and submitted to relevant international bodies.

"We are not here to talk about the past. We are here to build the foundations of Afghanistan's future — together, as Afghans, regardless of our differences."

Participants and Community Representation

The Sacramento session was notable for the diversity of its participants. Attendees included former government officials, academics, community leaders, religious scholars, women's rights advocates, and youth representatives — reflecting Mili Mehwar's commitment to inclusive dialogue that cuts across ethnic, regional, and political lines.

Representatives from the Afghan-American community in California played a particularly active role, both as participants in the working groups and as hosts of the event.

Next Steps

The outcomes of the Sacramento dialogue will be compiled into a formal report, to be released within the coming weeks. The next national dialogue session is expected to be held in a yet-to-be-announced city, with planning already underway.

Mili Mehwar extends its gratitude to all participants, volunteers, and community members who made the Sacramento session possible, and reaffirms its commitment to continuing this vital process of national dialogue wherever Afghans are.
