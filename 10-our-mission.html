About Us

Our Mission

The vision, mission, core values, and founding pillars of Mili Mehwar — the Afghan National Movement.

Our Mission Pioneers Constitution Why Mili Mehwar?

Our Founding Statement

"Afghanistan's salvation lies not in the hands of foreign powers, but in the organised, principled unity of its own people."

— Mili Mehwar, founding declaration, 2019

Our Vision

Mili Mehwar envisions a free, sovereign, and democratic Afghanistan — a nation governed by the rule of law, where every citizen regardless of ethnicity, religion, or gender enjoys equal rights and full participation in the country's political and social life.

We believe that Afghanistan's future cannot be decided by any single faction, foreign government, or armed movement. It must be built through an inclusive political process that reflects the genuine will of the Afghan people — at home and across the diaspora.

A just and lasting peace in Afghanistan is only possible when Afghans themselves hold the pen that writes their constitution, their laws, and their future.

Our Mission

Mili Mehwar was founded in 2019 with a single, unwavering mission: to bring together Afghans across political, ethnic, and geographic divides and to create a structured, principled platform for national dialogue.

We do not seek power for its own sake. We seek a political environment in which power is exercised accountably, transitions happen peacefully, and the fundamental rights of all Afghans — especially women and minorities — are guaranteed and protected.

Our work is grounded in three concrete commitments: facilitating structured national dialogue, educating and empowering Afghan civil society, and advocating internationally for an Afghan-led, Afghan-owned political solution.

Core Values

Every action Mili Mehwar takes is guided by a set of core values that we hold to be non-negotiable — in our internal organisation, in our public work, and in the way we engage with all Afghans regardless of their background.

National Unity

We reject ethnic, sectarian, and regional divisions as tools of political manipulation. Every Afghan has an equal stake in the nation's future.

Sovereignty

Afghanistan's political future must be determined by Afghans alone. We oppose all forms of foreign interference in the country's internal affairs.

Inclusion

No solution for Afghanistan can be legitimate without the full participation of women, youth, minorities, and civil society in all political processes.

Rule of Law

Durable peace requires institutions — an independent judiciary, a free press, and a constitution that protects every Afghan citizen equally.

Accountability

We hold ourselves to the same standards we demand of others. Our governance, finances, and decision-making processes are transparent to our members.

Principled Dialogue

We believe competing visions for Afghanistan's future can only be reconciled through structured, respectful, and evidence-based national dialogue.

Strategic Pillars

Mili Mehwar's work is organised around three strategic pillars that together form the foundation of our national programme.

1. National Dialogue

Since 2019, we have convened national dialogue sessions — Meli Maraka — in London, Berlin, Birmingham, Cologne, and Sacramento. These sessions bring together academics, political figures, civil society leaders, religious scholars, and diaspora community members to develop shared recommendations for Afghanistan's political future. Each session produces a formal report submitted to relevant international bodies and Afghan political stakeholders.

2. Civil Society Education

We operate a growing network of book clubs across Europe and North America, bringing Afghans together to engage critically with ideas about governance, democracy, human rights, and national identity. These clubs are not merely reading groups — they are schools of civic engagement, designed to prepare a new generation of Afghan civic and political leaders.

3. International Advocacy

Mili Mehwar engages actively with international organisations, foreign governments, and multilateral institutions to advocate for an Afghan-led, inclusive, and rights-respecting political settlement. We believe that sustained international attention and principled engagement remain critical to ensuring Afghanistan does not disappear from the global agenda.

Our Founding & History

Mili Mehwar was founded in 2019 by a group of Afghan academics, lawyers, civil society leaders, and professionals living in the diaspora. Confronted by decades of war, repeated political failures, and the growing threat of a complete collapse of the Afghan state, the founders resolved that Afghans themselves had to take ownership of the search for a solution.

In the years since its founding, Mili Mehwar has grown to over 14,000 members across more than six countries, convened multiple national dialogue sessions, and established itself as one of the most organised and principled Afghan civil society movements in the diaspora.

The movement does not operate with a central headquarters. It is a network — distributed, resilient, and rooted in the Afghan communities wherever they have settled around the world.
