Pioneers

The founding members, leaders, and dedicated individuals who built Mili Mehwar from the ground up.

Our Mission Pioneers Constitution Why Mili Mehwar?

Showing 6 pioneers

Ahmad Shah Ahmadi

Chairman

Founder — Leadership

Ahmad Shah Ahmadi is a legal scholar and political analyst who has dedicated over two decades to the study of Afghan constitutional law and transitional justice. He co-founded Mili Mehwar in 2019 and has served as its chairman since inception, convening national dialogue sessions across Europe and North America. His academic work has been published in international journals on governance and post-conflict state-building.

Constitutional Law Political Analysis Transitional Justice

Maryam Noori

Deputy Director

Founder — Leadership

Maryam Noori is a human rights advocate and public policy specialist based in London. A founding member of Mili Mehwar, she leads the movement's women's rights and education portfolios. She has represented Afghan civil society at the United Nations, the European Parliament, and numerous international conferences. Her work focuses on ensuring Afghan women have a central role in any future political process.

Human Rights Women's Rights Public Policy

Dr. Omar Rahmani

Founding Member — Economics

Dr. Omar Rahmani is an economist and development specialist with a PhD from the London School of Economics. He joined the founding group of Mili Mehwar and leads the movement's economic policy working group. His research on Afghanistan's post-conflict economic reconstruction has informed both academic debate and practical policy development within Afghan diaspora institutions.

Economics Development Policy Research
Martyr

Prof. Hasan Kargar

In Memoriam

Founding Member — Martyr of the Movement

Professor Hasan Kargar was a beloved educator, poet, and civic organiser who was among the founding members of Mili Mehwar. A lifelong champion of Afghan culture and education, he dedicated his final years to building bridges between the diaspora and the homeland. He passed away in 2022 after a short illness. His memory and his vision continue to guide the movement's work.

"The pen is the weapon of the oppressed, and the classroom is the battlefield of the just."

Education Literature Civic Leadership

Sara Wali

Founding Member — Diaspora Engagement

Sara Wali is a community organiser and social entrepreneur who has spent over a decade building Afghan civic networks across North America. She was instrumental in establishing Mili Mehwar's presence in the United States and Canada, and coordinated the Sacramento and upcoming Vancouver dialogue sessions. She leads the movement's book club programme and youth engagement strategy.

Community Building Youth Engagement Social Enterprise

Khalid Hussain

Secretary General

Leadership — International Affairs

Khalid Hussain is a diplomat and international relations specialist who serves as Secretary General of Mili Mehwar. Based in Berlin, he manages the movement's relationships with international organisations and foreign governments, and represents Mili Mehwar in multilateral forums on Afghanistan. He previously served in a senior advisory role at a prominent European think tank focused on Central Asian affairs.

Diplomacy International Relations Central Asia
