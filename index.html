Our Purpose

Why Mili Mehwar?

Afghanistan today faces a crisis of leadership, legitimacy, and national identity. Mili Mehwar was founded on the belief that lasting change can only come through organised, principled political action rooted in Afghan values.

We are not a government in exile. We are a movement of Afghan citizens — inside and outside the country — committed to building the institutional foundations for a free, just, and sovereign Afghanistan.

National Sovereignty
Restoring Afghanistan's independence, free from foreign interference and internal tyranny.
Unity Across Divisions
Building bridges across ethnic, regional, and political lines in service of the nation.
Democratic Governance
Advocating for inclusive, representative, and accountable institutions for all Afghans.
